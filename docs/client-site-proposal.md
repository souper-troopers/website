# Souper Troopers Website Proposal

## What this is
This is the first simple proposal for the new Souper Troopers website.
It shows the key pages, primary visitor actions, and the homepage structure we recommend.

This is meant for Kerry to review and confirm before we build the first version.

- Home
- About

- Get Involved
- Donate
- Contact

---

## Homepage proposal
The homepage should do three things quickly:
1. Explain who Souper Troopers is.
2. Show the main ways people can help.
3. Guide visitors to the page that fits their goal.

### Sections

#### 1. Hero
- Headline: “We are all human. We all have a story. Together we are the change.”
- Subheadline: “Souper Troopers helps people move from crisis to dignity through food, work and community.”
- Primary button: Donate now
- Secondary button: Shop gifts + coffee
- Supporting link: Learn how we help people rebuild

#### 2. Visitor paths
Three clear cards:
- Support a person
- Partner with us
- Buy with purpose

Each card links to a focused page with the right actions.

#### 3. Impact snapshot
Use short proof points:
- People helped through CAST
- Partner organisations supporting the work
- Products that fund dignity-building programs

#### 4. How it works
A short “How we help” section:
- Support
- Training
- Impact

#### 5. Shop + Donate
A simple split module with:
- Shop: purpose-driven products
- Donate: direct support options

#### 6. Latest update
One short update or story that shows momentum.

#### 7. Contact / next step
A final call to action:
- Donate now
- Shop now
- Get involved

## Page outlines

### About
Purpose: explain the organisation, story, values and credibility.

Content:
- Mission and vision
- What we do
- How we do it
- Where we work
- Partners and supporters

### Our Work
Purpose: explain the key programs and outcomes.

Content:
- CAST journey
- Humanity Hub overview
- Participant pathway
- Impact metrics / stories

### Shop
Purpose: make the store a meaningful support channel.

Content:
- Why the shop exists
- Featured products
- How purchases help
- Shop support details

### Get Involved
Purpose: make it easy for individuals and businesses to take the right action.

Content:
Sections for:
- Individuals
  - Donate money
  - Donate goods
  - Volunteer
  - Buy products
  - Sign up for updates
- Businesses
  - Partner with us
  - B-BBEE / corporate giving
  - Donate goods
  - Sponsor programs

### Donate
Purpose: make giving simple and trusted.

Content:
- Donation options
- Preferred payment methods
- Impact of donations
- Quick contact for support

### Contact
Purpose: provide easy ways to reach the team.

Content:
- Email and phone
- Contact form or direct request
- Location / social links

---

## Why this design
This version keeps the site focused on the things people need most:
- donate
- support
- buy
- understand the mission

It avoids a complicated menu and instead directs visitors to the right path quickly.
