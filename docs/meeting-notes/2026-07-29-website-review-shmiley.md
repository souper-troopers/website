## Overview
Meeting on updating the Souper Troopers website to integrate Shmiley, the Pty Ltd income-generating
arm of the non-profit (owns Troopers Coffee and African Worry Dolls sales), aiming to reduce reliance on
donors. Discussion covered a clear, user-friendly site linking Souper Troopers and Shmiley products,
compelling personal-story and product content, online ordering, and a dual approach targeting both
donors and product buyers.

## Key discussion points
- Shmiley is a separate Pty Ltd entity, owned by the non-profit, for tax reasons — exists to upskill
  previously homeless people, create jobs, and generate income to reduce donor dependency.
- Shmiley products (Troopers Coffee, African Worry Dolls) should be integrated into the main site, with
  a clear and flexible online ordering system — framed as a real deliverable, not a "coming soon" note.
- Content strategy: heartfelt personal stories, short/impactful copy, and short compelling videos to
  convey impact — explicitly called out for African Worry Dolls' "unique stories."
- Messaging should also speak to the global South African diaspora as a potential support community.
- Dual approach: the site needs to work for both donors and product buyers, not just one audience.
