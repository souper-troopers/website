## 5. The shop needs restructuring around how the dolls are actually made
The most detailed discussion of the meeting, between Adrian, Kerry and Shan.

- **Keep the variations visible** (general male and female, keyring, brooch, magnet, angel, elf)
  rather than the old dropdown — Kerry: *"it just makes it more obvious the variations we've got."*
  But the **names need to be clearer**, e.g. "African Worry Doll — General Female".
- **Each variation gets a carousel of photographs of that variation**, instead of one image that
  mixes keyrings, magnets and angels together.
- ⚠ **It must be obvious that every doll is uniquely handmade and will not look exactly like the
  photograph.** Kerry: *"you're placing an order for the variation and not what it looks like."*
- **Add a custom option.** Customised dolls cost more and should carry a **minimum order (~10 of a
  variation)**, and route to a contact form rather than a quiet mailbox.
- **Wholesale**: a line saying *wholesale by appointment, contact us*, routed to a team member rather
  than only Kerry, *"rather than you missing an email"* (Adrian). The **price list stays off the
  site** — it changes too often.
- **The current product grid reads as cluttered** — Adrian preferred the old site's larger images
  without card borders; Hilton: *"simple is sexy."* Partly a photo problem (the current shots are
  old and wrong), partly the card treatment.
- Seasonal idea, from Kerry and Stephen together: a **topical banner** on the shop — Mandela Month,
  Christmas — and photographs showing **the dolls in use** (Kerry's wedding place settings), or the
  makers at work (Shan). Today the products *"hover in the middle of nowhere"* without context.
