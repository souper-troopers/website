## What this tells us about the current structure

- The site is currently organized around audiences: individuals, businesses, donors, shops, and impact.
- The About page and Humanity Hub appear to be the main storytelling sections.
- The shop is a distinct destination, separate from donate and support pages.
- Contact and donate are available, but the donation flow appears to be low-detail and dependent on email/direct deposit.

## What we think are the major visitor journeys so far

### 1. Donor / supporter journey
1. Land on home page or a landing path.
2. Understand Souper Troopers’ mission and what makes it unique.
3. See impact and proof points (CAST outcomes, partners, stories).
4. Find the easiest way to support: donate, buy coffee, donate goods, sponsor a project.
5. Complete an action or contact the team.

### 2. Volunteer / program supporter journey
1. Learn what Souper Troopers does and why it matters.
2. Understand the CAST journey and the Humanity Hub.
3. See opportunities to get involved: volunteer, join CAST, donate goods, offer training.
4. Contact the team or sign up through the right channel.

### 3. Product/customer journey
1. Arrive at Shop or Home.
2. Understand the gift/coffee product purpose.
3. Browse products and learn how purchases support the work.
4. Buy online or follow store instructions.

### 4. Business partner / corporate journey
1. Recognize Souper Troopers as a partner with measurable outcomes.
2. Identify B-BBEE / corporate giving benefits.
3. Learn how to donate money, donate goods, or partner.
4. Contact the team for a formal partnership.

### 5. Story / mission journey
1. Seek the organisation’s story: origin, values, approach.
2. Learn how Souper Troopers is different from other charities.
3. Discover partner and supporter network.
4. Validate credibility through metrics and case examples.

## Current gaps and hypotheses to validate

### Gaps
- Exact donation methods and preferred payment flows.
- Current product/catalog details and fulfillment process.
- Key metrics to surface on homepage.
- Current content assets and where they are stored.
- Who will update the site after launch.
- Timing for any campaigns or events.

### Hypotheses
- Primary actions are: donate, volunteer/join, buy, and contact.
- Homepage should be built around audiences, not just a general charity story.
- The site needs clearer evidence of impact and a stronger direct donation path.
- A small number of strategic pages can replace the current scattered page structure.

## Suggested next step

Create a one-page homepage concept that shows:
- main headline and mission statement
- three visitor paths (donate, join/support, shop)
- short proof/impact section
- link to About / Our Work / Donate / Shop

That will reveal the missing specifics faster than another round of generic questions.
