# Photos for the Humanity Hub services (Our Work)

**2026-09-24: three are now Souper Troopers' own** (marked below), chosen so that no participant can be
identified - a face beside "Recovery support" or "Housing assistance" would say something about that
person on a permanent public page, and consent (q19) so far covers names, not faces. The other four stay
stock until consent or face-free shots exist. The two Documentation images on the Drive are AI-generated
and must not be used as real photographs.

Placeholders until real photographs from the planned shoot are available. All are **CC0** on
Wikimedia Commons (public-domain dedication: free for commercial use, no attribution required),
chosen to show the service rather than any person in hardship. They are decorative on the page
(`alt=""`) - the card text carries the meaning, and stock photos must not be passed off as
Souper Troopers' own people or places. Resized to 1600px on the long edge; originals linked below.
